# Public datasets for Recce Step 0: drone and smartphone captures

Date of search: 2026-11-26, by a research subagent; the EuroSDR RPAS record (licence CC BY 4.0, 43.1 GB, Phase 2 9.9 GB) was re-checked against Zenodo's API afterwards. Method: web search plus fetch of dataset pages, repositories, Zenodo records and papers. Nothing was downloaded. Where a fact comes from a search snippet and not from a page I fetched, or a page was silent, the item appears under "Could not verify".

Already known and not re-checked here: OpenDroneMap sample sets (no ground truth), ARKitScenes (in use), ETH3D, Mip-NeRF 360, Tanks and Temples.

### Best three for Group A

1. **EuroSDR RPAS benchmark.** CC BY 4.0, surveyed checkpoints, GCPs, a TLS cloud and a road profile, and the same site flown with no RTK and with RTK. It is the only candidate where drone GPS quality is the experiment variable, so it answers the pose-prior question the way RC-P8 did for ETH3D and ARKitScenes. The 9.9 GB Phase 2 file keeps the first fetch small; the P1 flight reaches about 1,000 images.
2. **GauU-Scene V2.** CC BY 4.0 (stated in the paper), six scenes of 424 to 1,215 images with drone-log georeferencing and a LiDAR reference. Use it for the 1,000+ image and coverage-report tests. Its LiDAR alignment is fitted, so do not use it as an absolute-scale oracle.
3. **UseGeo.** LiDAR reference, ground-truth depth and 829 images at 7952 x 5304, small enough to run whole. Non-commercial and share-alike, so it suits internal prototypes and published results only, not shipped fixtures. Mill 19 is the fallback for a scale-only distribution test with no ground truth.

### Best three for Group B

1. **ScanNet++ iPhone part.** Only set with raw ARKit poses and iPhone LiDAR depth registered to sub-millimetre laser scans on 1,000+ rooms. Apply early because approval is by hand; confirm licence fit first, since commercial use is prohibited.
2. **LaMAR.** The only verified source of both iPhone ARKit and Android ARCore device poses, with laser-scan alignment. It tests the Android capture-app pose pipeline and metric alignment outdoors as well as in. Access is by form and sizes are unknown.
3. **MuSHRoom (iPhone part) for a small laser-referenced set, with ADVIO for ARKit versus ARCore drift.** MuSHRoom is one 36 GB download of ten rooms with a Faro mesh, but its licence is stated three different ways. ADVIO is 5 GB, parallel iPhone and Pixel tracks, CC BY-NC. Use ADVIO if only an Android versus iOS comparison is needed.

## Mapping to Recce needs

- Drone GPS as pose prior (RC-P8 style): EuroSDR RPAS phases 1 and 2 compare no-RTK and RTK priors against surveyed checkpoints. Mill 19 and GauU-Scene supply drone-log positions but no independent reference.
- Metric scale and frame checks: EuroSDR RPAS (GCP and checkpoint coordinates, TLS), ScanNet++ and MuSHRoom (laser meshes), MobileBrick (exact brick geometry).
- 1,000+ image distribution: EuroSDR P1 flight (about 1,000), Mill 19 (1,940 and 1,678), GauU-Scene scenes (up to 1,215), UrbanScene3D and SkyLume (far larger and non-commercial).
- Android device poses: LaMAR (ARCore), ADVIO (Pixel ARCore), Map-free (ARCore or ARKit, undistinguished), Objectron (ARCore or ARKit, undistinguished).
- Licence flag for everything above: only EuroSDR RPAS (CC BY 4.0) and GauU-Scene (CC BY 4.0 in the paper) are clearly permissive. Non-commercial or restricted: UseGeo, ScanNet++, UrbanScene3D, SkyLume (per paper), Map-free, ADVIO, egenioussBench. Unclear: MuSHRoom, H3D, UAVD4L, WildUAV, LaMAR data, Mill 19 data.
