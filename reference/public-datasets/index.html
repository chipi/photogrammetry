    Public datasets¶

    Until the owner shoots their own sets, development and the Step 0 prototypes (RC-brief §5) run on public datasets. This page lists which exist, what ground truth each carries, what the licence lets the project do, and which prototype each fits. The question it answers: which sets can we reuse easily, and which carry a wall.

    As of 2026-11-24.

    Legend:

    • [V url] read today on the dataset's own page, repo, Zenodo record or licence file, or measured today with an HTTP HEAD request or the Zenodo API. Page reads went through a summarising fetch tool, so a quoted licence name is the tool's reading of the page; re-read the licence text before any redistribution.
    • [U] not read today; from memory or from a search snippet. Verify before relying on it.
    • "unknown" means neither the page nor today's checks gave the value.
    • No dataset was downloaded. Sizes come from page text or HEAD requests.

    General SfM and MVS¶

    ETH3D high-res multi-view¶

    • What: 15 training scenes (420 images) and 12 test scenes (445 images), indoor and outdoor, DSLR. [V https://www.eth3d.net/datasets]
    • Camera: Nikon D3X, 6048x4032, raw NEF, JPEG and undistorted JPEG all provided; original camera model THIN_PRISM_FISHEYE. [V https://www.eth3d.net/documentation]
    • Ground truth: Faro Focus X 330 laser scans with colour, three versions (raw, clean, eval); poses in COLMAP text format. Stated accuracy: unknown, the documentation page points to the paper. [V https://www.eth3d.net/documentation]
    • Sizes (HEAD, 2026-11-24): multi_view_training_dslr_jpg.7z 5.05 GB; ..._undistorted.7z 5.89 GB; ..._raw.7z 13.04 GB; ..._scan_eval.7z 1.92 GB. [V https://www.eth3d.net/data/]
    • Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International. [V https://www.eth3d.net/]
    • Registration: none seen for downloads (direct HEAD requests succeed); the Submit link needs a login.
    • Fits: P1 (GLOMAP vs COLMAP registration error against GT poses), dense and mesh accuracy against laser GT, P7b RAW-to-set (the NEF files are the only public RAW set here with laser GT).
    • Limit: only 15 training scenes; the largest is a few dozen to a hundred images; not a P3 distribution test.

    COLMAP sample datasets¶

    • South Building (128), Gerrard Hall (100), Person Hall (330), Graham Hall (1,273 images, interior and exterior). Resolution, size, licence and ground truth are not stated on the page. [V https://colmap.github.io/datasets.html]
    • Download host is https://demuc.de/colmap/datasets/; the *.zip paths I guessed returned 404 today, so the file names are unknown. [V]
    • Fits: P1 smoke runs, Graham Hall as a mid-size (1,000+) registration set for P3. No GT, so registration rate and reprojection error only.
    • COLMAP software licence (BSD-3) is not the data licence; do not assume the data is BSD. [U]

    Tanks and Temples¶

    • 8 intermediate, 5 advanced, 6 training scenes; images are uniformly sampled frames from 4K video at 29.97 fps; training scenes carry laser-scan GT, COLMAP results, camera poses and alignment matrices. Download by Google Drive links or a Python downloader with MD5 check; no registration required. [V https://www.tanksandtemples.org/download/]
    • Licence page names CC BY 4.0, but its licence grant limits use to "non-commercial scientific research" and bars making the data available to third parties without written permission. The two clauses conflict; treat the data as non-commercial, no redistribution. [V https://www.tanksandtemples.org/license/]
    • Fits: P1 and splat quality (the 3DGS paper uses these scenes). Video frames, not RAW stills.

    DTU MVS¶

    • 124 scenes, 49 or 64 images each at 1600x1200, seven lighting conditions; structured-light point clouds and STL reference models as GT. Downloads: SampleSet 6.3 GB, Points 6.3 GB, Points_MVS 45 GB, Surfaces 28 GB, Cleaned 136 GB, Rectified 123 GB. [V https://roboimagedata.compute.dtu.dk/?page_id=36]
    • Terms: "freely available for research use", citation required. No named licence on the page. Commercial use unknown. [V same page]
    • Fits: object-scale dense accuracy. Controlled robot-arm rig, so it does not stress registration.

    Not read today¶

    BlendedMVS, Strecha fountain/herzjesu, 1DSfM, Photo Tourism, IMC PhotoTourism, Replica, EuRoC: the two URLs I tried for 1DSfM and the ETH local-feature page returned 404, and I did not search the rest. [U] Photo Tourism and 1DSfM are internet photos of landmarks with mixed copyright; assume research-only until read.

    Radiance fields¶

    Mip-NeRF 360¶

    • The scenes ship as 360_v2.zip, 12,535,427,936 bytes (HEAD 2026-11-24), last modified 2022-03-28. [V https://storage.googleapis.com/gresearch/refraw360/360_v2.zip]
    • The project page read today did not state the licence, per-scene image counts or resolution. The multinerf repo is Apache-2.0 and archived 2025-02-11; that licence covers code, not the photos. [V https://github.com/google-research/multinerf]
    • Fits: splat quality on held-out views. Unknown data licence means local development only.

    Not read today¶

    NeRF synthetic (Blender), Deep Blending: [U]. Both are synthetic or research-released; check licences.

    Indoor, robotics and pose priors¶

    ScanNet++¶

    • 1000+ indoor scenes; sub-millimetre laser scans, 33 MP registered DSLR images, iPhone RGB-D streams, 360 degree RGB-D panoramas added October 2025. [V https://scannetpp.mlsg.cit.tum.de/scannetpp/]
    • Access: create an account, submit an application, wait for approval, receive a personal download token. Terms of Use PDF shown after signup; commercial terms not read. [V same page]
    • Fits: P9 (room), DSLR vs iPhone comparison, P4 pose priors. Gated; use only after the owner reads the Terms.

    ARKitScenes¶

    • 5,047 captures of 1,661 scenes, iPhone/iPad LiDAR RGB-D with ARKit poses and LiDAR surface reconstruction; high-resolution data for 2,257 captures across 841 scenes. Download through download_data.py. [V https://github.com/apple/ARKitScenes]
    • LICENSE is an Apple custom licence granting a "personal, non-commercial" licence "for non-commercial purposes only" (the text read covers the Apple Software; it is the only licence in the repo). Questions go to ARKitScenes-license@group.apple.com. [V https://raw.githubusercontent.com/apple/ARKitScenes/main/LICENSE]
    • Fits: the closest public analogue of the owner's iPhone capture app (P10) and ARKit pose priors (P4, P9). Non-commercial: development use only.

    TUM RGB-D¶

    • Kinect 640x480 at 30 Hz, motion-capture ground-truth trajectories (8 high-speed cameras, 100 Hz). CC BY 4.0; code BSD-2-Clause; commercial use permitted. [V https://cvg.cit.tum.de/data/datasets/rgbd-dataset]
    • Fits: only the pose-prior and depth plumbing. Not photogrammetry quality imagery.

    Not read today¶

    Replica, EuRoC: [U].

    Objects¶

    • DTU: see above.
    • CO3Dv2: 5.5 TB full set, 8.9 GB single-sequence subset, 20 GB download chunks; codebase CC BY-NC 4.0. [V https://github.com/facebookresearch/co3d] Not a fit: size, NC, phone-quality video.
    • OmniObject3D, Google Scanned Objects, Stanford 3D Scanning Repository: not read today. [U]

    Aerial and drone¶

    OpenDroneMap sample sets¶

    • The OpenDroneMap GitHub organisation hosts at least 18 odm_data_* repos (apt, benchmark, caliterra, copr, glam, langley, pacifica, seneca, lewis, bellus, rv_nir, waterbury, toledo, aukerman, zoo, garfield_msp, helenenschacht, monterey_road_bend). [V https://api.github.com/orgs/OpenDroneMap/repos]
    • Aukerman, Seneca: licence CC0-1.0. [V repo licence API] images/ entries: aukerman 77, seneca 167, langley 200, benchmark 50 (counts of directory entries via the GitHub API, not verified as image counts). Camera, resolution and size: unknown. No ground truth.
    • Fits: aerial registration smoke tests and clone-and-go P3 trials at 100-200 images; too small for P3's 1,000+.
    • Not read: UrbanScene3D, DJI samples. [U]

    Underwater¶

    Public underwater sets with usable ground truth and a permissive licence are scarce. The best-known colour sets are non-commercial, and the RAW files are not in the public download.

    Sea-thru (Akkaynak and Treibitz)¶

    • Five sites D1-D5, about 1,100 images, with range maps in metres (from Metashape SfM); zero depth means no value. [V https://huggingface.co/datasets/bhowmikabhimanyu/seathru-dataset]
    • Images are linear PNG at 0.3x original size. The original RAW files are not in the download; the authors say to contact them. [V same page]
    • 38.4 GB; licence on the Hugging Face card is CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0. The Haifa page links Kaggle and says "RAW images" are included, which conflicts with the card; resolve by reading the Kaggle page. [V https://www.viseaon.haifa.ac.il/datasets]
    • Original resolution, camera model, colour-chart presence: not read; the colour charts claim is [U].
    • Fits: P8 colour against a reference target, range-dependent attenuation estimate. Non-commercial: development use only.

    SQUID¶

    • 57 stereo pairs, four sites in Israel (Red Sea: Katzaa coral reef 10-15 m, Satil wreck 20-30 m; Mediterranean: Michmoret, Nachsolim), depths 3-30 m. RAW, TIF, calibration and distance maps included. [V https://zenodo.org/records/5744037]
    • Zips: Katzaa 12.1 GB, Michmoret 16.9 GB, Nachsolim 10.5 GB, Satil 6.2 GB, 45.8 GB total. Licence CC-BY-NC-SA 4.0. [V same page]
    • Satil, read 2026-11-25 (RC-P12): Nikon NEFs of 7380 x 4928; the TIFs are sRGB-encoded (not linear) and clip red to zero on 78-99.9% of pixels, so colour work needs the RAW. Chart annotations (corners and patch centres) for right-camera images ship with the evaluation code (underwater-dataset-evaluation-undisorted-params.zip on the project page), not in the dataset zips; they sit a few pixels off the published undistorted TIFs. The calibration is a MATLAB stereoParameters object, unreadable outside MATLAB. [V RC-P12 README]
    • Only stereo pairs, so not a full SfM set; the distance maps are the value.
    • Fits: P8 scale and colour checks, RAW decode test for P7b-style handling underwater.

    SeaThru-NeRF scenes¶

    • Four scenes (IUI3 Red Sea, Curacao, Japanese Gardens Red Sea, Panama) per a search snippet; the repo ships images_wb/, sparse/0/ and poses_bounds.npy; Google Drive link; photo credit Matan Yuval. Image counts, resolution, RAW: not stated. Repo code Apache-2.0; the data licence is not stated on the page read. [V https://github.com/deborahLevy130/seathru_NeRF]
    • Images are white-balanced and COLMAP poses are included, so they test splat quality, not registration.
    • Fits: P8 splat quality on held-out views; the same set WaterSplatting evaluates on. [U for the snippet claim]

    Other underwater sets found¶

    • AquaMVS Example Dataset 2: 13-camera underwater rig, 10 synchronised frames per camera, independent ChArUco board ground truth, calibration including water-surface depth; 6.2 GB zip (8.6 GB uncompressed); CC BY 4.0; published 2026-07-02. Lab geometry, not a dive site. [V https://zenodo.org/records/21134749]
    • Submerged3D: 4 deep-sea wreck scenes x 20 RGB images at 720p; no ground truth stated; CC BY 4.0. Too small for a benchmark. [V https://zenodo.org/records/15482420]
    • Reefs4D: 21 PLY models of seven coral reefs at three time points; Zenodo licence id cc-nc; the file list I saw holds PLY files only, no source images. [V https://zenodo.org/api/records/7879992]
    • RapidBenthos (12 photogrammetric models and underlying images, 5-15 m, coral reefs), a Zenodo record of an Innsbruck synthetic two-media set, and a very-shallow-water coral set on Figshare surfaced in a search but were not opened. [U]
    • FLSea-VI (12 forward-looking visual-inertial sets, under 10 m, Kaggle): listed on the Haifa page; licence and size not stated. [V https://www.viseaon.haifa.ac.il/datasets]

    Not found or not checked¶

    No public underwater dataset with laser-scan or survey-grade GT on a full dive-scale 3D scene was found. Not checked: AQUALOC, the Underwater Caves dataset, VAROS, 100 Island Challenge, NOAA reef sets, TagLab sample data, wreck sets on Sketchfab. [U] The Eiffel Tower deep-sea set was not found.

    Recommended starter set¶

    Need Dataset Licence Why
    RC-P1 baseline registration (GLOMAP vs COLMAP) ETH3D high-res training scenes (JPG, 4.7 GB) CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 GT poses and laser scans, no registration, direct link
    Dense and mesh accuracy vs laser GT; RAW (P7b) ETH3D scan_eval (1.9 GB) plus NEF set (13.0 GB) CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 The only RAW-plus-laser public set found
    Splat quality, held-out views Mip-NeRF 360 (12.5 GB) unknown (code Apache-2.0) Standard splat benchmark; no GT, so read the licence before any release
    ARKit-style pose priors (P4, P9, P10) ARKitScenes Apple custom, non-commercial Closest analogue to the owner's iPhone capture; gated by licence terms; ScanNet++ is the alternative but needs approval
    Large set for P3 (1,000+) COLMAP Graham Hall (1,273 images) not stated Only 1,000+ set found; resolution unknown; no GT. Not high-res RAW, so it only exercises scale in image count; real P3 needs the owner's D850 set
    Underwater P8 SQUID (RAW, distance maps, 45.8 GB) plus AquaMVS Example 2 (6.2 GB) CC-BY-NC-SA 4.0 and CC BY 4.0 SQUID gives RAW and distances; AquaMVS gives refraction-aware GT under a permissive licence; SeaThru-NeRF scenes (Drive) for splat quality

    The best fits are restrictive: ETH3D, SQUID, Sea-thru and ARKitScenes all forbid commercial use. Only TUM RGB-D, AquaMVS, Submerged3D and the CC0 ODM sets are permissive, and none of them is a strong benchmark.

    Reuse rules¶

    • Local development and prototypes: allowed under every licence listed, including non-commercial ones.
    • Published benchmark results (numbers, plots, no data): allowed with citation; ETH3D, SQUID and Sea-thru require attribution and share-alike for adaptations.
    • Redistribute the data (including in this repo or on a shared artefact store): not under CC BY-NC-SA without matching licence and attribution; not at all for Tanks and Temples (written permission required), ARKitScenes (check the Apple terms), DTU, ScanNet++ (Terms of Use). Fetch from the source; commit only the download script.
    • Ship derived models (weights trained on the data, a product that embeds results): not for any non-commercial set. Treat a model trained or tuned on ETH3D, SQUID, Sea-thru, Tanks and Temples, ARKitScenes or CO3D as non-commercial unless counsel says otherwise.
    • Commercial-safe data: TUM RGB-D (CC BY 4.0), AquaMVS (CC BY 4.0), Submerged3D (CC BY 4.0), ODM CC0 sets.
    • Never share an ScanNet++ download token or copy approved data to other machines without reading the Terms.

    Not verified¶

    • Image resolution and size: COLMAP samples, Mip-NeRF 360 per scene, SeaThru-NeRF, ODM sets, Sea-thru original resolution, ScanNet++ and ARKitScenes sizes.
    • Licences not read: Mip-NeRF 360 data, SeaThru-NeRF data, ScanNet++ Terms of Use text, COLMAP sample data, DTU (no named licence), CO3D data terms (only the codebase licence read).
    • Datasets not opened at all: BlendedMVS, Strecha, 1DSfM, Photo Tourism, IMC, Replica, EuRoC, NeRF synthetic, Deep Blending, OmniObject3D, Google Scanned Objects, Stanford scans, UrbanScene3D, DJI samples, AQUALOC, Underwater Caves, VAROS, 100 Island Challenge, NOAA, TagLab, RapidBenthos, Sketchfab wrecks.
    • Whether Sea-thru RAW is on Kaggle (the Haifa page says yes, the Hugging Face card says no).
    • ETH3D laser accuracy figure, ETH3D registration wall beyond the download links I probed.
    • GT accuracy of ScanNet++, ARKitScenes and DTU beyond the descriptions above.
