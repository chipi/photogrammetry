Verdict rule

The charts

Identical colour charts at several ranges. Angle to grey: how far the grey patches are from grey (0° is neutral). Contrast: white against black on the chart; haze lowers it.

What the water adds, by range

The light scattered back by the water between the camera and a surface (backscatter), fitted to the darkest pixels at each range. A chart's black patch reflects almost nothing, so the fit should stay under it.
