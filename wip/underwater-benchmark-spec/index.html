    Open underwater benchmark: specification (2026-11-25)¶

    RC-P12's last clause: specify the open underwater benchmark from what the public sets lack. This page lists the gaps, each with the evidence that shows it, then the benchmark that closes them. Numbers come from RC-P12 and public datasets, section "Underwater".

    Recce starts from one case (see the brief, "Where this comes from"): 1,500 Nikon D850 photos of an underwater site that would not assemble on a laptop in a day. A benchmark that tests Recce underwater tests that case: a dive-scale set of high-resolution RAW photos from a camera in a housing, with ground truth for scale, shape and colour.

    What they lack¶

    1. Dive-scale multi-view sets with geometric ground truth. AquaMVS has a board in a tank; SQUID has stereo pairs; Sea-thru's ranges come from Metashape, a reconstruction rather than a measurement. No set found has hundreds of overlapping views of a site with independent scale and shape truth. RC-P12 shows why this matters: on SQUID Satil the eight stereo pairs barely overlap, only two small models register, and our dense depth keeps 0–1.1% of pixels.
    2. Colour references across many views. SQUID's charts sit in stereo pairs, so a correction can be scored but not built from a reconstruction of the same site; SeaThru-NeRF's images are already white-balanced. RC-P12 could not test range-based correction with ranges from our own reconstruction for this reason.
    3. Linear RAW with intact colour. Sea-thru ships linear PNGs at 0.3x, without RAW. SQUID ships RAW, but its TIFs clip red to zero on 78–99.9% of pixels, so anyone who uses the TIFs measures 37.9–40.8° from grey on every chart whatever their method does.
    4. Documented ports. AquaMVS models a flat water surface with the cameras in air. SQUID does not document its housing; our registration refines the focal length to 1.37–1.51 times the in-air value, which points at a flat port but is not confirmed. No set documents a dome port and where the lens's entrance pupil sits in it, which decides whether a pinhole model holds. On AquaMVS a pinhole calibration is off by −0.03% to −4.8% in scale depending on where the board is, against at most ±0.28% for the refractive model.
    5. Readable calibration. SQUID's calibration is a MATLAB stereoParameters object; its chart annotations sit a few pixels off its published undistorted images, because its evaluation undistorts on the fly. AquaMVS's corner pixels live on images undistorted with OpenCV's new camera matrix, not the calibration's K, which RC-P12 found only by reading their source.
    6. Size. The largest set with range maps (Sea-thru, about 1,100 images) is at 0.3x resolution and its ranges come from a reconstruction. Recce's founding case is 1,500 full-resolution RAW photos; distribution (RC-P7) and memory limits only show at that size.
    7. A permissive licence with truth. Of the sets with ground truth, only AquaMVS is CC BY; every set with colour references forbids commercial use.

    The benchmark¶

    Scenes¶

    Scene What Why
    S1 Site One wreck or reef site, 1,000–2,000 RAW photos with a DSLR in a housing behind a dome port, in overlapping passes at two or three distances Gaps 1, 2, 6: Recce's founding case
    S2 Flat port A 100–200 photo subset of S1's area through a flat port Gap 4: the same site under both port models
    S3 Action camera A GoPro pass over S1's area The owner's second underwater camera; registration of mixed sets
    S4 Calibration A ChArUco board photographed through each port in the water, at three or more distances Gaps 4, 5: in-water calibration with its own truth, as AquaMVS does in a tank
    S5 Dive set The friend's dive set with its Metashape result, if the friend agrees to publish it A real-world baseline against the commercial tool

    Ground truth placed at the site¶

    • Scale bars: two or more rigid bars with coded targets at a measured distance (tape or caliper, measured on land), seen from many views. Scored as scale error, as in RC-P9 and RC-P12.
    • Target network: six or more coded targets whose pairwise distances are taped underwater, giving a shape check independent of any reconstruction.
    • Colour charts: three or more identical charts at 1, 3 and 6 m from the main pass's camera path. Their patch values are measured above water under a known illuminant. This gives SQUID's test (charts at several ranges) in many overlapping views (gap 2).
    • Port record: port type and dimensions, and the lens's entrance pupil position relative to the dome centre, measured on land.

    Measures¶

    Every measure already exists in a prototype, so the benchmark adds no new scoring code.

    Measure From
    Registration rate, time and cost RC-P1, RC-P7
    Scale error on the scale bars; target-distance error; rigid RMSE RC-P9, RC-P12 (AquaMVS)
    Dense and mesh accuracy against the target network RC-P4
    Grey-ramp angle to grey, ramp contrast against range, chart-pair angle RC-P12 (SQUID)
    Splat quality on held-out views RC-P4

    Format¶

    • RAW files as they come from the camera, EXIF untouched; no derived TIFs in the release.
    • Calibration as plain JSON in OpenCV's convention (K, distortion, R, t, port parameters), readable without MATLAB.
    • Target and chart annotations in the pixel grid of the RAW files, with the code that produced them, so no annotation depends on an undistortion step the user cannot repeat.
    • Measured distances and chart patch values as CSV, with the instrument and its stated accuracy.
    • Licence CC BY 4.0 for the owner's own captures; S5 under whatever the friend agrees to.

    Open, for the owner¶

    • Whether to capture S1–S4, and when: one or two dives, plus a pool session for S4.
    • The licence for the owner's captures; CC BY 4.0 is the proposal.
    • Whether the friend's dive set (S5) can be published, or stays a private baseline.
    • RC-P12's "ranges from the reconstruction" clause moved to S1 (owner, 2026-11-26); S1 is where it is tested.
